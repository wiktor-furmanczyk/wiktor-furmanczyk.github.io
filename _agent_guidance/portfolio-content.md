# Portfolio content guidance for Codex

This file guides future edits to the public VeltoWeb portfolio. It is agent-facing repository documentation, not a page or copy to display on the site. Follow the repository's local `AGENTS.md` and portfolio workflow skill for source boundaries and publication rules.

## What the reader should understand

Write for a recruiter, a technical team, or a potential collaborator. The main message is Wiktor's practical experience in diagnosing technical problems, understanding systems and data flows, and improving processes. Apps and automation demonstrate what that experience can produce; they are not his whole professional identity.

- Present Wiktor as a technically experienced problem solver. He is not a professional software developer, and his value is not limited to prompting AI.
- Show that he can receive and diagnose a technical issue, work with technical teams, analyze data, improve or automate a process, and create a useful tool when needed.
- Connect business and technical perspectives. His own online sales and ventures help him recognize user and market problems; his technical work helps him find causes and practical solutions.
- State plainly that he uses AI assistance to create his own apps. Show that decisions about the problem, scope, system behavior, and validation are his. Do not suggest that he hand-coded features created with AI assistance.

## Facts and evidence

Ground experience claims in the user's direct statements or approved public material. Relevant facts include technical issues in e-commerce stores and integrations; collaboration with developers, technical departments, and clients; implementations; commercial data collection and analysis; and Wiktor's own online sales and ventures. Check the current public copy before repeating time-sensitive employment claims.

Do not invent job titles, employers, clients, metrics, years of experience, product capabilities, release dates, or outcomes. Separate demonstrated work from future capability. Do not imply that an independent project has commercial users or a public release without evidence. For project-specific facts and images, follow the `public-documentation/` source boundary in the local `AGENTS.md`; never use private application files or customer data.

## Form of the site content

- **First screen:** say what problems Wiktor solves and the kinds of systems and processes he understands. Lead with experience and contribution before showing projects or technologies.
- **Experience:** use concrete examples of technical problem solving, cross-team cooperation, work with data, and the perspective from running online sales. Keep the distinction between professional experience and personal projects clear.
- **Projects:** explain the problem, intended user, chosen solution, Wiktor's role, relevant technologies, current status, and next step. Present a working personal-use version, testing, planned work, and public release as different stages.
- **Roadmap:** use dates and status labels only when supported. Make completed milestones and plans easy to tell apart.
- **Calls to action:** point readers to relevant project details or the professional profile. Avoid competing buttons and labels with inconsistent meanings.

Write concise first-person copy in clear Polish. Prefer specific actions and results over tool lists, course lists, marketing language, and unsupported superlatives. Use scannable headings and paragraphs short enough for mobile reading. Keep Polish and English pages aligned in meaning, project status, and certainty, while translating naturally.

Preserve the site's visual system: readable hierarchy, consistent spacing, labels, buttons, and relevant media with useful alternative text. Do not add decorative content that makes the experience or project status harder to understand.

Before publishing, check that a reader can answer: what problems Wiktor has handled, how he works with people and systems, what he can do for a team, what each project actually does, and which results are current rather than planned.
